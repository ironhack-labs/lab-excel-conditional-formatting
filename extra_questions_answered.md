1. Changing National Insurance (Nat Ins) Rate
Impact on Employee:

Lower Rate: More take-home pay (higher Final Pay)

Higher Rate: Less take-home pay (lower Final Pay)

Impact on Employer:

Lower Rate: Reduced payroll costs for the company

Higher Rate: Increased payroll costs for the company

Example: If Nat Ins drops from 6.3% to 5%:

Employee gains ~1.3% more take-home pay

Company saves 1.3% on payroll taxes

2. Changing Tax Rate
Impact on Employee:

Lower Rate: Significant increase in take-home pay

Higher Rate: Significant decrease in take-home pay

Impact on Employer:

Indirect impact: May affect employee satisfaction and retention

Administrative: Payroll system updates needed

Example: If tax rate drops from 20% to 15%:

Employee gains 5% more take-home pay

Government receives less revenue

3. Changing Pension Contribution Rate
Impact on Employee:

Higher Contribution: Less take-home pay now, but more retirement savings

Lower Contribution: More take-home pay now, but less retirement security

Impact on Employer:

Higher Contribution: Increased benefits cost for company

Lower Contribution: Reduced benefits cost for company
